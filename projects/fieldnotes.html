THE LATEST ENTRIES

001 / EVERYDAY

The long way home

READ ENTRY +

The usual route is efficient. Three turns, one crossing, and a familiar door. Today I took a different street.

There was a window full of plants I had never noticed, a small bakery closing for the afternoon, and a patch of sunlight that made an ordinary wall feel like a place to stop.

Nothing remarkable happened. That was the point. Sometimes a little distance from routine is all it takes to notice where you are.

002 / MAKING

Leave room in the margins

READ ENTRY +

A page filled to every edge leaves little space for a second thought. The same can be true of a day, a design, or a conversation.

I have been leaving more things unfinished on purpose: a corner of a notebook, a gap in the calendar, a first draft that can still change.

A margin is an invitation. It says there is room for something you have not thought of yet.

003 / OBSERVING

A collection of small things

READ ENTRY +

A receipt used as a bookmark. A sentence overheard on the train. The particular blue of the sky just before the streetlights turn on.

These things do not ask to be collected. Most of them disappear if you do not write them down.

This journal is a place to keep a few of them. No grand theory, just a habit of looking a little closer.
